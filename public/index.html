        How It Works

        The analyzer works in 4 simple steps:

        1

        Select a Concept

        Choose a concept you want to test yourself on, like Arrays, Loops, or OOP.

        2

        Explain It In Your Own Words

        Speak or type your explanation as if you are teaching it to a friend.

        3

        Analyze Your Understanding

        The system checks your explanation against a knowledge base and calculates a score.

        4

        Learn and Practice

        Read the feedback, learn from the explanations, and practice with questions.

        About

        This is a student-focused learning tool designed to identify knowledge gaps and improve concept understanding. It helps students test their own knowledge and get feedback on what they know and what they need to learn.

        Objectives

        • Improve concept understanding
        • Identify knowledge gaps
        • Provide simple explanations
        • Encourage active learning
        • Help students test themselves
